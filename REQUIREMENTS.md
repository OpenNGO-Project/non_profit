# REQUIREMENTS.md — Non Profit

Requirements for `non_profit`. This file is the requirement-level source of
truth: requirements, `DOCUMENTATION.md`, `HOW_TO.md`, and the code must
match. Update this file whenever requirements change; keep requirement IDs
stable (never reuse a retired ID — mark it "Retired:" with the reason).

Status: retrofitted on 2026-07-17 from current code, existing docs, and
archived agent sessions (opencode/Claude/Codex). Describes what the app is
required to do today, not a historical design spec. Current package version:
`16.20.0`.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

`non_profit` is a shared fundraising and membership substrate: a hard fork of
Frappe's Non Profit app for Frappe v16 with Swiss fundraising additions
(yearly donation receipts, Swiss QR-bill donation slips, donor thank-yous) and
reworked membership semantics (B2C + B2B, Households, subscription billing).
Downstream presentation, analytics, demo, and dispatch apps consume its neutral
DocTypes, services, and provider hooks without reversing the dependency.

Explicitly out of scope / dependencies:

- Payment-provider integrations (checkout, webhooks, callback verification)
  live in gateway apps such as `payrexx_integration`, never here.
- Client-specific UI, seeding, branding, and presentation flows belong to
  downstream site or presentation apps; this app stays generic.
- ERPNext is a required app (`hooks.py required_apps`); its `Task`, Customer,
  Payment Entry, Subscription, and Bank Transaction doctypes are always
  present. `good_connector` integration is optional (defensive imports) and
  the app must remain installable without it.
- The app does not provide legally approved Swiss tax-certificate wording or a
  Donation/public-presentation currency field contract (see §4). Household
  roll-ups separately derive Company currencies only to prevent mixed-currency
  addition.

### 2.4 Donation Receipts — REMOVED 2026-07-31

The legacy submittable `Donation Receipt` (+ child `Donation Receipt Item`) was a
second Bescheinigung model next to `Donation Tax Receipt`. Operator decision of
2026-07-31 (`LETTER_DISPATCH_CONVERGENCE_PLAN_2026-07-31.md`, Phase 2b): one
Bescheinigung remains. The DocTypes, their Print Format, the delivery settings
fields, and `Donation.receipt` were removed outright in 16.10.0; the patch
`non_profit.patches.drop_legacy_donation_receipt` drops the leftover rows and
tables. **REQ-NP-RCP-01 … REQ-NP-RCP-04 are superseded and withdrawn**; their
subject matter is covered by REQ-NP-STR-01 … REQ-NP-STR-10. The
`NPO-DRCPT-` naming-series prefix is retired and must not be reused.

### 2.4a Donation Tax Receipts (Spendenbescheinigungen)

- REQ-NP-STR-01: `Donation Tax Receipt` is the annual Swiss donation tax receipt: exactly one document per Donor, calendar `tax_year`, and Company (enforced by a unique database index), autonamed by its tax year as `NPO-STR-{tax_year}-{#####}` through the controller rather than the creation-year `{YYYY}` token, carrying `total_amount`, `donation_count`, and a service-maintained `donation_details` JSON list of `{donation, date, amount}`. It is a plain document (not submittable) with statuses Draft / Issued / Cancelled, a `Draft` default, `issued_on`, cancellation audit fields, a `language` Select (`de`/`fr`/`it`/`en`, default `de`), a read-only `email_sent_on` Datetime, and free-text `remarks`. Its currency is the issuing Company's `default_currency` — CHF on a Swiss site, EUR on a German one — and a receipt may not be issued in any other currency; `tax_year` must lie between 2000 and 2100. It is available to System Manager and Non Profit Manager and is searchable by donor name and tax year. Since 16.10.0 it is the **single** Bescheinigung of the app. [Trace: `non_profit/non_profit/doctype/donation_tax_receipt/`; Tests: `non_profit/non_profit/test_tax_receipts.py`]
- REQ-NP-STR-02: Receipt insertion, deletion, and changes to `donor`, `donor_name`, `tax_year`, `company`, `currency`, `status`, issue/cancellation/email audit fields, `total_amount`, `donation_count`, and `donation_details` may occur only through the receipt service. The guard uses one module-private capability sentinel compared by object identity (the `good_direct_mail/services/guards.py` pattern), so direct Draft insertion, Desk/import edits, and request-supplied truthy flags are rejected. Operators may still edit `language` and `remarks`. [Trace: `doctype/donation_tax_receipt/donation_tax_receipt.py`; Tests: `non_profit/non_profit/test_tax_receipts.py`]
- REQ-NP-STR-03: `generate_receipts(company, tax_year)` is a permission-gated POST endpoint (Donation read plus Donation Tax Receipt create/write/delete) that verifies current-user read access/User Permission for a Company that has a default currency and aggregates only qualifying Donations — submitted (`docstatus 1`), `paid = 1`, same Company, dated inside the calendar year, `amount > 0`, and with a Donor — into one Draft receipt per Donor. ## 4. Explicit Decisions and Constraints

Intentional behaviors a reader might otherwise mistake for bugs (documented in
`DOCUMENTATION.md` and `HOW_TO.md` unless noted):

- **One Bescheinigung, and it is Swiss (decided 2026-07-31).** The German-law
  `Donation Receipt DE` format and the whole legacy `Donation Receipt` model
  are gone. The single Bescheinigung is `Donation Tax Receipt` with the seeded
  German `Spendenbescheinigung` Print Format: CHF, calendar tax year, Swiss
  confirmation wording, address-free header (Letter Head owns the addresses).
  The format is operator-owned once edited; deployments needing a legally
  reviewed local variant edit it in place. (`DOCUMENTATION.md`
  "Donation Tax Receipts", `HOW_TO.md` "Spendenbescheinigungen")
- **A Verdankung is not a Bescheinigung.** `Donation.thank_you_sent` records a
  per-donation thank-you. A dedicated `Donation Acknowledgement` (Verdankung)
  document is planned as Phase 5 of
  `LETTER_DISPATCH_CONVERGENCE_PLAN_2026-07-31.md`; do not re-model it as a
  second Bescheinigung.
- **Currency is a presentation assumption, not a contract.** The base `/donate`
  page and confirmation labels display EUR; the seeded `Donation Thank You DE`
  template formats the Donation Company's default currency (create-only, so
  pre-16.20.1 sites keep their operator-owned copy); `Donation Slip CH`
  displays CHF. Donation has no currency field, so production sites must
  provide one approved currency-aware presentation flow.
- **Compatibility facades are a telemetry hold, not forgotten debt.** Legacy
  payment/invoice dotted paths warn through the `non_profit.compatibility`
  logger and stay until ≥ 90 days of telemetry plus one zero-call release
  cycle (audit finding B28; `legacy_payments.py`, `HOW_TO.md`).
- **One Swiss QR-bill engine per bench.** `swiss_qrbill.py` is a public,
  provider-neutral dispatch seam, not a renderer. A downstream app registers
  the complete payment-part implementation through
  `non_profit_qr_bill_svg_providers`; without one, Donation printing continues
  without a payment part. This prevents a weaker second renderer from drifting
  on QRR, IBAN validation, address structure, or recipient language.
- **`Membership.company` was removed on purpose.** It was never the member's
  business relation; resolve organisation context via `Membership.member ->
  Member.customer -> Customer` (`patches/remove_membership_company`).
- **The Household child table IS the history.** Leaving a household means
  setting `to_date` on the Contact row rather than deleting it; `Member.household` /
  `Donor.household` are read-only role projections that change through
  Household rows or the canonical `add_person_to_household` service (Household
  audit findings 2026-07; repo `AGENTS.md` "Household Model").
- **A Household gets at most one Customer projection, and only on demand.** It
  is created only at an explicit financial trigger through a dedicated service,
  never through Quick Entry. Household is deliberately not an ERPNext Party
  Type and not a `customer_type` value, and two people must never share one
  Person-subject Customer — joint activity uses a Household-subject Customer.
  This is why `get_or_create_donor_for_household()` creates no Customer.
- **NPO roles are never `Customer Type` values.** One Customer can hold several
  roles (Member, Donor, Volunteer), so those are separate DocTypes rather than
  Customer classifications. An ERPNext `Company` must never represent an
  external member, donor, partner, Customer, or Supplier, and finer
  organisational forms (association, foundation, public body) belong in the
  separate `legal_form` field, not in `customer_type`.
- **Supplier-only parties stay Supplier-only.** A payable-only person or
  organization must never receive a Customer just to satisfy the identity
  model. Customer and Supplier remain separate accounting masters, and a Party
  Link is not proof of one identity — which is why Supplier carries no
  `npo_household` field.
- **Email is matching evidence, never an identity key.** It supports duplicate
  review and deterministic reuse but does not name records. `Volunteer` still
  uses `autoname: field:email` and that is known, gated debt: moving it to a
  stable naming series needs a controlled migration that preserves incoming
  links, and changing an email must never rename an existing Volunteer.
- **`Donor.subject_type = "Anonymous"` is a reserved system subject, not an
  ambiguity fallback.** It carries no Contact, Household, or organization and
  exists only as an accounting/attribution sink; identified people are never
  merged into it. Neither `recipient_selection` nor `correspondence` has an
  Anonymous branch, so such a Donor resolves to no canonical subject and is
  excluded fail-closed — keep it that way until an explicit anonymous-giving
  service is designed.
- **The party-model plan is archived, not lost.** `PARTY_MODEL_REFACTOR_PLAN.md`
  described a Customer-regime redesign (Membership Ask, Donation Economic
  Receipt, protected-lifecycle registry, Payment Entry via Customer) that was
  never shipped; the live regime still references Donation directly and
  registers Member/Donor as receivable Party Types. The plan was moved to
  `/workspace/development/archived/` in 16.7.0 after its still-current
  decisions were ported into this section, REQ-NP-MEM-01, REQ-NP-MEM-09, and
  `DOCUMENTATION.md`.
- **The Contact-based Household child has an ordered migration.** The 16.3.0
  pre-model patch converts `Household Member` rows before schema sync and the
  post-model patch finalizes identity classification and projections. It fails
  closed before conversion when a legacy role cannot be mapped unambiguously;
  never bypass that stop by deleting old child rows.
- **Household lock-order convention.** Mutation paths always acquire
  canonical Contact locks in deterministic order before querying conflicting
  Household Person rows; ordinary derived-field reads during role validation
  are deliberately non-locking (Stage 1 party model, 2026-07).
- **Next actions are real Tasks, by design.** The `next_action*` fieldnames
  are kept read-only/derived so the pipeline list and reports keep working off
  them; pre-existing free-text values were migrated to Tasks by patch.
- **Stricter Payment Entry validation is intentional.** Stale drafts and
  alternate accounts are rejected even where historical data violates the
  invariants; the known over-allocated Donation `NPO-DTN-2026-00436` (excess
  246) is flagged for manual review and must not be auto-repaired (audit
  worklist wave 2, `AUDIT_REMEDIATION_WORKLIST_2026-07-14.md`).
- **Mock payments are not a payment path.** The guest `mock_pay` endpoint is
  development-only, POST-only, and gated by two site flags; production payment
  confirmation must come from a real gateway integration.
- **Member dashboard intentionally omits Bank Account.** Bank details belong
  to the linked ERPNext Customer, not to Member.
- **Guided Organization Contacts are correspondence people.** An Organization
  Member is backed by an Organization Customer and has no canonical
  `Member.contact`. An optional named human Contact is linked to the Customer
  and Member through standard Dynamic Links; the foundation/company itself is
  never represented as a Contact. Organization addresses are operational
  address links, not postal-consent metadata.
- **ERPNext loyalty test fixtures are neutralized on purpose.** Setup disables
  `auto_opt_in` only for ERPNext's known test fixtures so NPO Customer saves
  do not show "Multiple Loyalty Programs found"; real Loyalty Programs are
  untouched.
- **Reserved settings fields.** `Non Profit Settings` fields
  `stale_interaction_days` and `lapsed_major_months` are defined but
  intentionally not yet wired to any behavior.
